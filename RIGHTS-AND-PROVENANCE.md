# Rights and Provenance

## Purpose

This repository is designed to preserve authorship, provenance, and version history for the GEI research project.

## Original project material

Original GEI-authored research, taxonomies, schemas, interpretations, hypotheses, documentation, and other original project material should carry clear provenance and authorship metadata where appropriate.

## Third-party material

Third-party texts, quotations, images, datasets, translations, and research remain subject to their respective copyright, licenses, and terms. Their inclusion as a source or citation does not transfer ownership to GEI.

## Provenance rule

When incorporating external material, record enough bibliographic information to identify where the material came from and what role it plays in the research.

## Version history

Git history is part of the project's provenance record. Significant changes should be traceable rather than silently replacing earlier research states.

This document is a provenance policy, not a grant of rights or legal advice.
