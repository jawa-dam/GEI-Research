GEI-Research V1.0.10
GEI Research Decision → Execution → Observation Engine

Canonical chain:
INVESTIGATION → DECISION → EXECUTION → OBSERVATION → RESULT → OUTCOME

Epistemic rule:
CONNECTED ≠ SUPPORTED ≠ CORROBORATED ≠ CONFIRMED

A decision authorizes an action; an execution records what was done; an observation records what was observed. Results and outcomes must be recorded explicitly and may be inconclusive.
