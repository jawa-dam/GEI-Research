# V1.0.14 — Immutable Research Ledger & Chain-of-Custody Engine

## Purpose
V1.0.14 extends V1.0.13 from **drift detection** to **historical state reconstruction**.

**V1.0.13 asks:** Has this record changed since the trusted baseline?

**V1.0.14 asks:** Can we reconstruct the recorded research states, their order, their digests, and their provenance without silently rewriting history?

## Architecture

```text
CANONICAL RECORD
      ↓
PROVENANCE BASELINE
      ↓
CONTENT DIGEST
      ↓
LEDGER STATE
      ↓
PARENT STATE + PARENT DIGEST
      ↓
CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY
      ↓
INTEGRITY CHECK
      ↓
AUDIT RESULT
```

## Non-negotiable rule

> **No system component may silently rewrite scientific/research history.**

A correction, revision, challenge, retraction, restoration, or new finding is represented as a new state and a new provenance event. Historical states remain historical states.

## Epistemic firewall

A valid chain demonstrates **traceability and integrity of recorded states**. It does not demonstrate that a hypothesis is true, that an interpretation is correct, or that evidence is sufficient.

**CONNECTED ≠ SUPPORTED ≠ CORROBORATED ≠ CONFIRMED**

## Tamper model

```text
CHANGE ONE CHARACTER
        ↓
SHA-256 CHANGES
        ↓
BASELINE MISMATCH
        ↓
DRIFT DETECTED
        ↓
CHAIN/AUDIT REQUIRES ATTENTION
```

The tamper simulation is intentionally illustrative. It changes an in-memory copy and never writes the altered state to the canonical dataset.
